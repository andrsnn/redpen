# New project kickoff

Ask the user these questions and write the answers into the project's `OUTLINE.md`. Keep it to one short exchange.

1. **Goal.** What is the one sentence the reader or audience should walk away with?
2. **Audience.** Who is it for, how much time do they have, and what should they do or know afterward?
3. **Content.** What are the real facts, steps, sources and examples? Anything not listed stays out.
4. **Off limits.** What must never appear (names, numbers, private details)?
5. **Existing material.** Is there a doc, notes or old deck to port from?
6. **Shape.** One line per page: `NN · short name — the claim the page makes`.

Get a yes on the shape before building pages.
